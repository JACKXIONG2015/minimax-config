### How to Use Customs Data
1. **Forward search**: Search by competitor name to find their shipments (reveals customers and suppliers)
2. **Reverse search**: Search by a known customer to see if competitor also supplies them
3. **Product search**: Search by HS code to find all importers of a specific product category
4. **Supplier overlap**: Compare competitor's suppliers with your own to identify shared dependencies

## Confidence Level Guidelines

When reporting findings, assign confidence levels based on data source quality:

- **High confidence**: Data from official filings, multiple corroborating sources, direct observation
- **Medium confidence**: Data from reputable third-party tools (SimilarWeb, Semrush), single-source customs data
- **Low confidence**: Estimates, inferred data, single unverified source, outdated information

Always flag low-confidence findings and recommend verification steps.
